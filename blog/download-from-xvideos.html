How to Download Videos from xVideos

October 20, 2026 · ImageGet Team

xVideos doesn't offer a save button, so the usual workarounds are screen-recording the player or pushing the link through a converter site — both slow, and both leave you with a worse copy than the original. ImageGet skips that. Paste the video link, let the app analyze the page, choose one of the versions the site serves, and keep the file in your photo library.

Step 1: Open ImageGet and tap Video Download

Open ImageGet and tap Video Download on the main screen. Every video site the app supports lives in this section.

Step 2: Choose xVideos Download

Tap xVideos in the list. The screen switches to xVideos mode and waits for a video link.

Step 3: Copy the link from the video page

Go to the video you want and copy the URL of that page — not the category, the search results, or the home page. Those pages don't point at a single video, so there's nothing for ImageGet to pull out. On a computer, copy from the address bar; in a mobile browser, use the share sheet.

Step 4: Paste it into ImageGet

Paste the link into the field and tap Analyze. ImageGet loads the page and reads the media behind it, handing you the video file rather than a preview or a still frame.

Step 5: Choose a version and download

ImageGet shows whichever versions the site serves, each labelled with its resolution and file size. Pick one, tap Download, and it saves to your photo library — ready to watch offline, no connection or browser needed.

Tips for downloading from xVideos

  • Free downloads — the free plan gives you 3 video downloads; upgrade to Premium for unlimited downloads and an ad-free experience.
  • Match the file to your screen — on a phone, a mid-range resolution looks close to the top one at a fraction of the size. Save the highest version only when you plan to watch on something bigger.
  • Watch the file size — ImageGet lists the size next to each version, so check before you commit on a long video.
  • Only save what you have the right to keep — xVideos hosts a mix of free, premium and third-party licensed content. Check the site's terms before you download.
