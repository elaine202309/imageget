Getting Started with ImageGet in 5 Minutes

August 28, 2026 · ImageGet Team

New to ImageGet? This quick guide gets you from download to your first saved batch in about five minutes.

What is ImageGet?

ImageGet is an all-in-one photo saver for the web. Log into Instagram to save images and reels as you browse, or enter a URL to gather every image on the page — then review, filter, and save them straight to your photo library.

Step 1: Download the app

Get ImageGet from the App Store. It's free to download, and you get 5 free downloads to try it out. If you like it, upgrade to Premium for unlimited saving and an ad-free experience.

Step 2: Save your first batch

  1. Log into Instagram, or copy the URL of any page with images you want to keep.
  2. Open ImageGet and paste the link.
  3. Tap Go and watch it gather every image on the page.
  4. Review the grid, then tap Save All.

Step 3: Explore the key features

  • Instagram & Reels — log in and save images and videos in one tap.
  • One-Tap Batch Download — save every image on a page at once.
  • Smart Filtering — keep only larger images and skip tiny icons and thumbnails.
  • Background Operation — queue multiple pages while you keep browsing.
  • Full-Screen Preview — zoom and swipe to review images up close.
  • Built-In Browser — browse directly inside the app, with bookmarks.

A quick tip

Use the built-in browser to visit your favorite sites and save images without ever leaving the app. Bookmark the sites you use most, and they'll always be a tap away.
