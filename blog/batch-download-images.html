How to Batch Download Images from Any Web Page in One Tap

August 26, 2026 · ImageGet Team

Saving images from the web used to mean right-clicking every single picture — or worse, taking screenshots and cropping them later. ImageGet changes that. Paste a URL, and the app gathers every image on the page in a single tap, so you can review and keep them all at once.

Why batch download beats saving one by one

When a page has a dozen images, saving them individually is slow and error-prone. You end up missing a few, or accidentally grabbing low-quality thumbnails. Batch downloading does the busywork for you: one action collects everything, then you decide what to keep.

How it works in four steps

  1. Copy the URL. Open the page — a photo gallery, a forum thread, a news article — and copy its link.
  2. Paste it into ImageGet. Open the app, paste the link, and tap Go. ImageGet scans the page and gathers every image on it.
  3. Review the images. All the images appear in a clean grid. Tap any one for a full-screen preview, and pinch to zoom before you decide.
  4. Save everything. Tap Save All to send the whole batch to your photo library in one go.

Pro tip: filter out the small stuff

Not every image on a page is worth keeping. Icons, avatars, and tiny thumbnails can clutter your library. Turn on Smart Filtering and set a minimum size — ImageGet will automatically skip the small stuff and keep only the larger images you actually want.

Pro tip: queue up multiple pages

Working through several pages? Queue them up and let ImageGet work in the background while you keep browsing. No need to wait — your images will be ready when you are.
