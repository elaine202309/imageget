Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Overview

ImageGet ("we", "our", "the app") is a tool for collecting and saving images from web pages you provide. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using ImageGet, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

  • Web content you provide. When you paste a URL or browse a site inside the app, ImageGet loads that page to discover the images on it.
  • Images you save. Images you choose to keep are written to your photo library, only after you grant the required permission.
  • Usage data. Anonymous, aggregated usage and diagnostic information may be collected to help us improve the app.
  • Advertising identifiers. The free version may display ads. Ad providers may use a device advertising identifier to serve and measure ads.

ImageGet does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, or other personal identity information.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core service: fetching and saving images at your request.
  • To maintain and improve app performance and reliability.
  • To serve advertising in the free version of the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Photo Library Access

ImageGet only accesses your photo library to save the images you explicitly choose to keep. The app does not read, upload, or share your existing photos without your action. You can revoke photo-library access at any time in your device's Settings.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app may rely on third-party services for advertising and analytics. These providers have their own privacy policies and may collect information as described in their policies. Where required, we rely on your consent for personalized advertising.

6. Data Retention

Downloaded images remain in your photo library until you delete them. Anonymous usage and diagnostic data is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above.

7. Children's Privacy

ImageGet is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Your Choices & Rights

  • You can grant or revoke photo-library access at any time.
  • You can limit ad tracking through your device's privacy settings.
  • Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app or on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@imageget.app.
